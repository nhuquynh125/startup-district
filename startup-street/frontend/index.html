Every Decision Matters

Prices, stock, staff and surprises. Small choices add up to a profit or a loss.

Set the price

Too high and customers walk by. Too low and you lose money on every sale.

Manage your stock

Running out costs sales. Over-ordering costs cash.

Handle surprises
